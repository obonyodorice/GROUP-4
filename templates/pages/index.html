F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with BrightPath

01

What does BrightPath do?

BrightPath is a digital agency that helps small businesses and startups build and grow their online presence, with website design and development, branding and graphic design, digital marketing, SEO and business digital solutions.

02

Who do you work with?

We focus on small businesses and startups that want to present themselves professionally online and reach more customers.

03

How does a project work?

Every project follows four steps: understanding your needs, planning the solution, design and development, and review and delivery. You can read more on our Our Approach page.

04

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. We design websites to be responsive, so they adapt to phones, tablets and desktop computers.

05

How can I start a project?

Send us a message using the contact form below, or use our contact details, and tell us about your business and what you would like to achieve.
