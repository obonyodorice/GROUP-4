Project Overview

This is a demonstration project created by BrightPath to show the kind of online store we can design and develop. It is not a real client project. It illustrates how an e-commerce website can be laid out so customers can browse products, view details and complete a purchase, and it focuses on design and layout.

The Goal

To show how a small business could sell its products online with a website that is clear, easy to use and works well on both mobile and desktop.

Our Approach

We would start by understanding the business and its products, then plan the store structure, design the pages, and develop and review the site before launch. This is the same process we follow for every website.

Key Features

The features this demonstration store is designed to include.

Product Browsing and Categories

Products are organised into clear categories so shoppers can browse and find what they need.

Product Detail Pages

Each product has its own page with images, a description and the price.

Shopping Cart

Shoppers can add products to a cart and review their selection before buying.

Checkout Interface

A simple, clear checkout layout that guides shoppers through placing an order.

Responsive Design

The store layout adapts to both mobile and desktop screens.
